# Owner onboarding coverage

Ask these progressively. Do not present the entire list in one turn.

## Purpose and audience

- What should the twin help someone understand about your professional work?
- Who is the intended audience?
- Is the result private, internal, public-candidate, or mixed?
- Which downstream format or system will consume the bundle?

## Privacy and retention

- Which source categories may the configured model provider process?
- May selected private content be sent to that provider for this purpose?
- How long should interview transcripts be retained?
- How should selected source files and derived candidates be retained?
- Which employers, clients, projects, metrics, or personal topics are restricted?

## Identity and authority

- What must the twin never answer?
- What authority must it never imply?
- What should it say when evidence is weak, missing, or disputed?
- Which contact or next-step path is approved?
- Which commitments, negotiations, scheduling, or representative actions are always forbidden?

## Publication

- Do you want private compilation only, or a separate public-candidate review later?
- Which source types do you have publication rights to use?
- Which third-party names or material must never appear?
- Who must approve final wording and visibility? The default and required answer is the owner.

## Interview controls

Confirm that the owner can say `skip`, `pause`, `private`, `retract`, or `delete` at any time, and
that no transcript statement is published directly.
